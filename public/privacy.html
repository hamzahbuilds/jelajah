Privacy

Jelajah is a private trip planner for small groups. Last updated 8 November 2026.

What we store

  • Your account: name, email address and a hashed password.
  • What you and your group add to a trip: plans, places, notes, expenses, documents and photos.

Sign in with Google

If you choose "Continue with Google", we receive only your Google account id, name and verified email address (scopes openid email profile). We use them to sign you in and to match you to your Jelajah account. We do not get access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data, and we never post anything on your behalf.

AI planning

When you use "Plan with AI", the text, links and screenshots you send are passed to an AI provider to draft your plan. Nothing else from your account is sent.

Sharing

Trip content is visible only to members of that trip, to people you share a link with, or to everyone if you publish it as a public guide. We don't sell your data or show ads.

Deleting your data

Ask your organiser or an admin, or email us, to remove your account and the data linked to it.
